Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Rush!. We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, enjoyable, and positive experience while playing our game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle and safeguard information when you install and play Block Rush! on your mobile device.

By downloading, installing, or playing the game, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and collect only what is strictly necessary to run the game:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, or photos. No account or login is required.
  • Local Storage Only: Your high scores, sound, music, and vibration settings are saved only on your device. This data is never sent to any server and is removed if you uninstall the app.
  • No In-App Purchases: The game is 100% free with no paid items, subscriptions, or financial transactions.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data: Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may automatically process basic non-personal technical data (such as device model, OS version, and crash logs) to display ads and keep the app running reliably.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, we display advertisements via Google AdMob. Because our game is designed for players aged 3 and above:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: All ad requests are marked as child-directed in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy.
  • G-Rated Content Only: All ads are restricted to general, family-safe audiences (MaxAdContentRating: G).
  • Non-Personalized Ads: We only serve contextual ads. We do not allow personalized ads, behavioral tracking, or user profiling.
  • No Advertising ID: We have explicitly removed the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission from our app.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Families Ad Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Protecting young players and family privacy is our top priority:

  • The game is family-friendly and strictly adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13 (or any user).
  • The game contains no chat, no social media links, and no external web links.
  • Parents and guardians can contact us at any time with questions or to request the deletion of any data.

5. Data Deletion

Because all game data (scores and settings) is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.
